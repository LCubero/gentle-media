## Format-specific composition

- 1920×1080 (16:9): parent transcript takes visual priority; rail/overlay can sit at right. Keep titles and subtitles at least 10% inside outer edges; never rely on screenshot microtext.
- 1080×1920 (9:16): recompose, do not simply crop the master. Stack enlarged parent/transcript, then isolate a task, agent card or captured-diff detail one at a time. Keep subtitles above platform controls and within a conservative 10% safe area; check title/subtitle collisions on a phone-sized preview.
- Use the GIF and PNGs as historical reference frames from the pinned source revision, not proof of today's live UI. Animate transitions with abstract geometry, not false clickable UI. Design for reduced flash and adequate contrast.

## Editorial timing

The six lengths are 150 + 300 + 330 + 360 + 390 + 270 = **1,800 frames**, exactly 60.000 seconds at 30 fps. The final SRT out-point `00:01:00,000` is the exclusive end of frame 1799. Keep caption blocks readable (roughly 2–5 seconds each); do not stack both language tracks in one deliverable.
